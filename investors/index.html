05—Gates

Five gates, from golden sample to scale.

  1. G1

    Lock the product

    Golden sample, CAD, bill of materials, final weigh-in

  2. G2

    Clear the launch

    Certification, battery safety, QA, service plan

  3. G3

    Place the PO

    Supplier terms, staged inspection, freight and customs

  4. G4

    Prove Dubai

    Deposits, acquisition cost, conversion, return and service load

  5. G5

    Scale UAE

    Repeatable economics, B2B pilot evidence, replenishment
